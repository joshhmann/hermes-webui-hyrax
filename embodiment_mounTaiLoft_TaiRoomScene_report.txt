---------------------
- `mountTaiLoft` (/frontend/src/embodiment/mountTaiLoft.ts): Mounts the Synthesis Loft UI, manages the host container, scene, and UI controls. Instantiates TaiRoomScene, sets up controls (camera, lighting, workbench), and handles destruction.
- `TaiRoomScene` (/frontend/src/embodiment/TaiRoomScene.ts): Main 3D scene class handling geometry, navigation, VRM model, lights, procedural room/fixtures, and loading room manifest (roomObjects.json).
- Both rely on `tai-room.css` for UI/chrome and on the operator/debug panel `RigDevelopmentPanel.ts` for advanced diagnostics/workbench functions.
